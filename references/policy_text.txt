stop future use of the relevant non-essential technologies;
update the preference record; and
remove or disable relevant technologies where technically possible and legally required.
Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful where valid consent existed at the time.
Withdrawing consent should be as easy as giving it.

You may object through:
the Cookie Preferences interface;

another clearly identified mechanism.

respect your choices;
demonstrate compliance;
investigate complaints; and

the consent categories change;

legal requirements change;

another refresh is reasonably necessary.
